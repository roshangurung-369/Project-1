Frequently Asked Questions

Can I participate in more than one game?

Yes! You can register for up to two different sports, provided their match schedules do not overlap. Please complete a registration step for each sport you want to try out.

What is the deadline for registration?

All registrations must be submitted by Friday at 5:00 PM. No late entries will be accepted so we can finalize the tournament brackets in time.

Do I need to bring my own equipment?

We provide basic tournament equipment like footballs, basketballs, and tennis nets. However, you must bring your own personal gear like athletic running shoes, shin guards, and tennis rackets.

What happens if it rains on game day?

Indoor sports (like Basketball) will proceed exactly as planned. Outdoor matches will either be delayed or moved to alternative indoor spaces if available. Check the announcement board on morning of the event!
